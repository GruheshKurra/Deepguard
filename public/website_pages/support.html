Frequently Asked Questions

🔒 Is my data safe?

Yes! DeepGuard processes all images and videos entirely on your device. Your media is never uploaded to any external server. Analysis results are stored only on your device and in your personal iCloud account.

🎯 How accurate is the detection?

DeepGuard uses advanced machine learning models, but no AI detection system is 100% accurate. Results should be considered as probabilistic assessments and not definitive conclusions.

📱 Which devices are supported?

DeepGuard requires iOS 17.0 or later and works on iPhone. The app is optimized for devices with Apple Neural Engine for faster AI processing.

☁️ How does iCloud sync work?

When enabled, your analysis history syncs across all your Apple devices using your personal iCloud account. This data is encrypted by Apple and only accessible to you.

🗑️ How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings within the app and tap "Clear All History" to delete all your analysis history from your device and iCloud.

🎥 What video formats are supported?

DeepGuard supports common video formats including MP4, MOV, and M4V. Videos are analyzed frame-by-frame for comprehensive detection.
